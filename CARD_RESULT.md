# K-123 — do-not list (recorded before drawing)

- Do not use airbrushed gradients, rainbow/neon highlights, glossy plastic brass, or bloom around every control. Use the approved warm palette and top-left light.
- Do not fill borders with decorative vines, repeated emblems, random scratches, or texture noise. Keep text fields quiet and wear at handling edges.
- Do not mix pixel densities, anti-aliased outlines, or incompatible corner geometry. Author at 1x; enlarge exactly 3x with nearest-neighbor sampling.
- Do not bake letters, numerals, or labels into any asset. Speed icons use one/two/four pips; the seal has a blank text centre.
- Do not invent screen layouts or repaint the game scene. Use the supplied real screenshots at their original dimensions for comparisons.

Research read before drawing: Docs/GDD/UI_Art_Spec_v1.md; https://alexheffernan.dev/projects/the-last-light (practical observations: excess colours, softened edges, noisy detail, inconsistent registration); https://www.jp.square-enix.com/octopathtraveler/about/ (official HD-2D reference). These are art-quality checks, not claims that visual artifacts can identify image provenance.
