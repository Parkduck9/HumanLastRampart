# K-110 — Anti-generic-art constraints (written before generation)

- Do not smooth worker skin or leather into glossy gradients; keep discrete warm light, midtone and dark shadow clusters.
- Do not omit mouths, blue irises, eye highlights, or distinguishing middle-aged hair/beards at native resolution.
- Do not use floating decorative gears, ambiguous hand/bar contact, or upright weightless pushing poses.
- Do not mix pixel densities or blur outlines; use a single native grid and integer nearest-neighbor exports.
- Do not wash the real courtyard in fog or neon; keep the screenshot camera/layout and use bounded amber burst rays.

Research read before drawing: https://www.theguardian.com/games/2025/jun/26/video-game-developers-using-ai-even-when-they-arent-stamina-zero and https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/11408 . The former discusses glossy finish and incoherent lighting as perceived AI tells; the latter explains directional sprite shading and avoiding pillow shading.
