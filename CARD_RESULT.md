# K-122 — Card result

## Card-specific do-not list (written before drawing)
- Do not cover the lantern cage or road in orange bloom; keep the flame crisp and deliver ground illumination as a separate restrained decal.
- Do not use airbrushed gradients, plastic metal, or inconsistent pixel sizes on the iron, stone, navy cloth, and brass emblem.
- Do not let rails, pole mounts, or crossbars melt into decorative clutter; preserve a functional, readable construction and a stable base in every frame.
- Do not invent a fantasy courtyard or change the camera, wall layout, scale, or colors of the supplied game screenshot.
- Do not turn the beacon emblem into illegible ornamental filigree; preserve one lighthouse/lamp silhouette and a few clear rays at small sizes.

Research read before drawing: https://runway.com/resources/why-do-ai-images-look-weird (generic polished rendering, object construction, and consistency); https://saint11.art/pixel_art_articles/article5/ (controlled edge treatment and banding). These inform the avoid list; visual traits alone do not prove AI provenance.
