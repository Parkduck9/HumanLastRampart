Card K-087 - connecting EARTH WALL and HILL pieces for grid placement (user feedback 2026-10-20, items 12/13 in Docs/GDD/Playtest_Feedback_20_v1.md).

Decided rules (user):
- Terrain grid cell = 5 m. A placed earth wall or hill always covers exactly TWO cells.
- Rotation in 45-degree steps: straight (2 side-by-side cells, horizontal or vertical) or diagonal (2 cells touching at a corner). Diagonal pieces must look CLOSED - no visible gap at the shared corner (the game also blocks movement through that corner).
- Pieces placed next to each other must look like ONE continuous wall / one continuous ridge (auto-connection by neighbours, like autotiles).
