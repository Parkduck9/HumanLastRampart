Card K-089 - PREP YARD pre-rendered background for 2.5D free walking (user feedback 2026-10-20, item 18 in Docs/GDD/Playtest_Feedback_20_v1.md).

Idea (from a reference video the user liked): one painted background image seen from a FIXED camera; the game puts an invisible 3D ground plane and a few invisible box colliders behind it, so the hero sprite walks left/right AND forward/back inside the picture, casts a shadow on it, and is hidden behind foreground objects cut out of the picture. Style: Octopath Traveler-like HD-2D pixel art, same art direction as COMMON.md.

Current yard (read only): Game/Assets/_Project/Meta/PrepYard.cs, PrepEntry.cs, background Game/Assets/_Project/Art/UI/Resources/ScreenArt/prep.png, K-082 prep characters Docs/References/codex_k082_v1/. The hero is ~73 px tall at native scale (D-081).

Make:
1. Background: inner courtyard of the castle at dusk-warm daylight, camera ~30 degrees downward, wide panorama 3840x1080 (the camera pans horizontally). Stations clearly readable as places, left to right: wall repair (tools, stone pile, a breach being patched), barracks (door, bunks/recruiting table), trap workshop (workbench, spikes, barrels), shop (merchant stall), stairs up to the wall (exit to next battle). Walkable ground in front of all of them with some depth (the hero can walk "into" the picture ~2-4 m).
2. Walkable mask: same size PNG, white = ground the hero may stand on, black = blocked.
3. Occluders: separate transparent PNGs (same canvas size, aligned) of foreground things the hero can walk BEHIND (pillars, stall roof posts, barrels, banner poles).
4. Ground geometry for the engine (manifest.json): horizon y, pixel rows of the nearest and farthest walkable lines, pixels-per-metre at those two rows (or camera pitch + FOV), and for each station: name, door/interaction point (x,y) in pixels, and a suggested trigger box (pixel rect on the ground).
5. A 1-frame mockup with the hero (from codex_k065_v1 / k070) standing near each station at correct scale, with a simple shadow, so scale and depth read correctly.

Output per COMMON.md: Docs/References/codex_k089_v1/, README.md (Korean), review page _HTML/6_그림_화면/codex_K-089_정비마당배경_v1.html (pan through the panorama, toggle mask/occluders).
